## The bet

Give the knowledge and the tool away; sell the guidance and the outcome. In 2027 Explora earns through the Practitioner and Educator Programmes and the Sprints, not through software sales.

## Product: Explora V2

1. **Examples Database.**
   - What it holds: 181 case studies and 1,000+ nature's solutions.
   - Open, under a non-commercial licence.
   - Stays at examples.learnbiomimicry.com.
   - Its job:
     - search traffic
     - email sign-ups
     - trusted cases for the connector
   - Three free reads, then email.
   - No million-mechanism target, no contribution system, no patent layer.
2. **Explora Connector for Claude.** Tools are released in curriculum order, one at a time:
   - **Release 1:** Project Finder 1.0 (per the v0.3 brief) by 8 Jan 2027.
   - **Release 2:** find nature's solutions. This starts only after 60% of Xenopus learners have used the Finder.
   - **Then:**
     - mechanism to design principle
     - concept builder
     - Educator curriculum builder

   Access:
   - Free tier with email sign-in.
   - Learner tier included for 12 months with programmes and Sprints.
   - No subscription until a third of learners still use it monthly at month three.
3. **Biomimicry Skill.** One file teaching Claude the five steps (Scope, Discover, Abstract, Create, Evaluate) plus the Life's Principles check. Free with email.
4. **Offers that carry it:**
   - Programmes, with the toolkit included.
   - Sprint Two.
   - Free live demo "Biomimicry with Claude" (12 Jan 2027).
   - Company pilot ($7,500, sold by calls only).
   - Short Course Set.

## Build rules

- Build only the knowledge and method layer. Claude is the model and the app. No own chat product; the Lovable build is retired as a product.
- Every tool maps to a curriculum step and is opened in a live session.
- The next tool starts only when the last one is in use.
