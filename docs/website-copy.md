### What is Explora?

Explora is an open map of how nature solves problems. Each entry explains one strategy from a living thing, in plain English:

- **How it works**, in three simple steps

- **Who in nature** uses it, from lotus leaves to termite mounds

- **Where it's been used** in real products, buildings and processes

- **How sure we are**, with a confidence level and the evidence behind it

It's free to read, built for search engines and AI, and checked by people who practise biomimicry for a living.

### I need to…

Start with what you need to do. We'll show you how nature does it.

- Keep things cool (or warm)

- Keep surfaces clean and dry

- Find, hold and clean water

- Be strong but light

- Stick, grip and join

- Move through air or water with less effort

- Make materials kindly and turn waste into value

- Organise teams, systems and places

### From problem to spec in four steps

Explora follows one journey, from a messy challenge to something your team can build.

1. **Find a problem worth solving.** Sharpen the challenge, find its root causes and list what a solution must do.

2. **Explore nature's solutions.** Search by function and shortlist the strategies with the best evidence.

3. **Design concepts.** Combine nature's strategies with your context into three or four concepts.

4. **Engineer specs.** Score each concept against your needs, set target values and plan the first test.

Steps 1 and 2 are free. For steps 3 and 4, our Team Sprint takes your team through them with an Explora mentor.

R&D, product and sustainability teams with a problem that conventional thinking hasn't cracked. For example:

3. **Three or four concepts.** Each with its design principle, likely materials, a way to make it and the existing technology closest to it.

4. **A spec for the winner.** Every concept scored against your needs, target values, a prototype and test plan, a first patent check and a Life-friendly score.

Your team also learns the method as they go, so the next problem doesn't need us.

### SEO notes

- **One page owns each search.** Google usually shows one page per site for a search, so the Biomimicry for Claude page owns "biomimicry for Claude". The home page supports it: the phrase in its title tag and a section that links to the page using those exact words. Together they give Google a clear signal without the two pages competing.

- **Main search for the home page:** "biomimicry database" and "nature's solutions"; "biomimicry for Claude" second.

- **Main search for Team Sprint:** "biomimicry consulting" and "nature-inspired innovation for R&D".

- **Questions sections** get FAQ structured data, so they can show up in Google and AI answers.

- **Live counts** (solutions, organisms, papers) come from published entries at build time, so they never go stale.

- **Copy rules:** no em dashes; "re-" words written with the RE in brackets; plain English.
